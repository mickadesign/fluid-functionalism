month: 2026-07
title: Card, MobileDrawer, 4 components on both primitives, and Lucide-only icon installs
---

Radix or Base UI for 4 more components, and 4 changes that need a migration, icons among them.

## New

- [Card](/docs/card): A compositional card in 13 parts, from `CardMedia` to `CardFooter`, borderless by default. `CardGroup` stacks cards, sets them inline, or lays them in a grid, with fluid hover that finds the nearest card across rows and columns. (#21)
- **MobileDrawer**: A slide-in navigation drawer with a scrim, a focus trap, and scroll lock, in both flavors: `mobile-drawer` or `base/mobile-drawer`. (af6f0eb, c57b11a)

## Breaking

- [Scrollbars](/docs/scrollbars): `ScrollArea` drops its edge fades and chevrons, along with the props that set them. Remove `scrollFade`, `cueSize`, and `chevron` from your call sites. (2a88947, a7f87c2)

  ```diff
  - <ScrollArea className="h-64" cueSize="tight" chevron={false}>
  + <ScrollArea className="h-64">
  ```

- [Select](/docs/select): `SelectContent` drops `scrollFade` along with the edge fade it turned off. (2a88947)

  ```diff
  - <SelectContent scrollFade={false}>
  + <SelectContent>
  ```

- [ThinkingSteps](/docs/thinking-steps): `ThinkingStep` drops its unused `index` prop, and `delay` sets the content fade. Remove `index` from your steps. (24b22ab)

  ```diff
  - <ThinkingStep index={0} label="Reading the brief" />
  + <ThinkingStep label="Reading the brief" />
  ```

- **All components**: Installs ship Lucide icons only, and the providers stop binding the `I` and `R` keys in your app. Pass `icons` to `IconProvider` to swap any slot for another library. (5964735, 21a5785)

  ```diff
  + import { CaretRight, MagnifyingGlass } from "@phosphor-icons/react";
  - <IconProvider defaultLibrary="phosphor">
  + <IconProvider icons={{ "chevron-right": CaretRight, search: MagnifyingGlass }}>
  ```

## Updated

- [Motion](/docs/motion): `spring.moderate` drops its bounce to `0`, so dropdowns, tabs, and drawers land without overshoot. (a001d30)
- [Button](/docs/button): `asChild` keeps the background, spinner, and icons on your element in both flavors, and the tertiary border shrinks with the fill on press. The page adds a playground and documents `active`. (522f4ce, #20, c01ae1a)
- [Dropdown](/docs/dropdown): `DropdownMenu` opens the list as a popup in both flavors, with typeahead, `Escape` to close, and focus back on the trigger. `MenuItem` takes `disabled`, and the inline list reads as a group, not a menu. (e9b184f, 8cb2408, efcc5ce)
- [Select](/docs/select): Ships in both flavors, with a popup that flips at the viewport edge and jumps to an option as you type. Picking a value holds the menu open 300ms so the check draws, and the highlight no longer slides in from the old value on reopen. (289a104, e9bb55e, #23)
- [Slider](/docs/slider): `steps={[0.1, 0.5, 0.7, 1.1, 1.3]}` snaps the thumb to those values only, and arrow keys walk the list. The thumb carries the slider's label for screen readers. (4e7b50b, efcc5ce)
- [TabsSubtle](/docs/tabs-subtle): Ships in both flavors as 1 tab stop: arrow keys move focus, `Enter` or `Space` selects. Tabs no longer submit a surrounding form, and the focus ring stops clipping on the first and last tab. (f2d822d, f1c511e, 3252f63)
- [ThinkingSteps](/docs/thinking-steps): Ships in both flavors on a collapsible, so screen readers hear whether a step is open. Installs carry the shimmer keyframes the active step needs. (24b22ab, ba829c5, caab5a5)
- **All components**: Labels trim to cap height, so the padding you set is the space you see, and every focus ring reads `--focus-ring`, falling back to `#6B97FF`. (67f1439, 3505a3e)
- **Inspect**: Every docs preview gets an Inspect toggle that draws rulers, padding, gaps, and font metrics over the demo. (#21)
